ABOUT BREMADE

Creativity Has No Borders.

BREMADE is a Zambia-based multidisciplinary creative agency with roots in the Copperbelt, operating from Kitwe and Ndola while extending its creative services to Lusaka and beyond.

We believe great creativity shouldn't be limited by location. Whether you're a growing business in Kitwe, an established brand in Ndola, or a company in Lusaka, we're ready to bring your ideas to life.

Through branding, graphic design, brand management, digital marketing, advertising, videography, and creative direction, we help businesses build their presence, connect with audiences, and stand out in an increasingly competitive world.
